# Global direction expansion

Turn a rough production note into one detailed, reusable instruction that every
shot must follow. The goal is to remove ambiguity before generation so the model
cannot drift.

## What to make concrete

- Subjects and objects: exact construction and mechanism, silhouette and
  proportions, number and arrangement of parts, color, material, condition.
- Wardrobe: garments, layers, fit, palette, accessories, footwear.
- Environment: architecture, geography, fixed landmarks, time of day.
- Lighting: key direction, color, contrast, sources.
- Look: palette, film/lens feel, grain, aspect ratio, framing rules.
- Continuity: what must stay identical across every shot, and what must never
  change.
- Prohibitions: the specific drift to prevent, stated as a rule.

## Rules

- Prefer specific, checkable wording over adjectives.
- When a detail is unspecified, choose one concrete default and state it as fixed;
  do not leave it open.
- Do not invent story events, characters, or dialogue; this is a directive, not a
  scene.
- Keep it compact enough to attach verbatim to every shot.
